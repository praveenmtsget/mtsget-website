Magic Basket

Skip the Lines. Shop Hands-Free.

Meet Magic Basket—the ultimate smart shopping companion featuring a seamless mobile app and an autonomous basket designed to transform your in-store retail experience.

Download the App

The Problem: Traditional Shopping is Broken

Navigating crowded grocery aisles, dragging heavy carts, and waiting in long checkout queues wastes your valuable time. Traditional retail hasn’t kept pace with modern life—until now.

The Solution: Enter Magic Basket

Magic Basket combines intelligent software with autonomous hardware. As you walk through the store, your Magic Basket follows your path or syncs directly with your smart app, automatically tracking your items as you select them.

  • Autonomous Tracking: Advanced sensors detect items as they are placed inside, automatically adding them to your digital cart.
  • Instant Mobile Checkout: Skip cash registers entirely. Pay securely through the Magic Basket app on your phone and walk straight out the door.
  • Smart Recommendations & Lists: Sync grocery lists from home, get real-time store mapping, and discover active deals on the fly.

How It Works

1. Grab & Sync

Open the Magic Basket app at the store entrance and pair it with your designated autonomous basket.

2. Shop Normally

Pick your items off the shelf and drop them into the basket. Watch your digital total update instantly on your screen.

3. Checkout & Go

Confirm your digital cart, complete a secure one-tap payment, and exit seamlessly.
